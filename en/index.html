The perfect flatmate doesn’t exist. The one you get on with does.

Uniflat connects students who move away for university and are looking for flatmates. Students only: no landlords, no agencies. For every person it tells you how compatible you are, why, and even where you might not see eye to eye.

How it works

Free. On iPhone in April 2027, in Milan, Bologna and Rome.

Today you meet your flatmates on the day you move in.

You find a room, you sign, you carry your bags up. Only then do you learn who’s on the other side of the wall: whether they study at night, smoke indoors, or turn the kitchen into a party every Saturday. The landlord doesn’t tell you, and often doesn’t know.

How it works today

  1. You find the room
  2. You sign the contract
  3. You meet whoever’s there

With Uniflat

  1. You find the people
  2. You look for a place together
  3. You sign knowing who with

A number that explains itself.

Answer ten questions about how you really live. For each one, say how much it matters to you. Uniflat compares your answers with other students in your city and shows you a percentage, with the reasons written next to it.

  1. Tell us how you live

    Tidiness, hours, quiet, parties, guests, smoking, pets. Plus interests, area and budget.

  2. Browse people, one at a time

    The most compatible come first. Tap the card to see what you share and where you differ.

  3. You choose each other, and the chat opens

    Only when the interest is mutual. Nobody can message you unless you chose them too.

The questions we get most

All questions
How much does it cost?

Nothing. Uniflat is free: no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads.

When does it launch, and where?

In April 2027, on iPhone, in Milan, Bologna and Rome together. If you join the list we’ll write to you on the day we open in your city.

What about Android?

It comes after iPhone, if enough of you ask for it. Join anyway: the list tells us that too.

How is the percentage calculated?

We compare your answers about living together with the other person’s, weighting each one by how much it matters to you. Then we add interests, area, budget, timing and age. The reasons are always next to the number.

Who sees my profile?

Only students in your city who use Uniflat. You can pause your profile whenever you like, and it disappears from other people’s feeds.
