The questions we get most

All questions
How much does it cost?

Nothing. Uniflat is free: no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads.

Where does it open?

In 18 university cities, one at a time. Join and complete your profile: when we open your city we’ll write to you, and your chats with the people most compatible with you will already be there.

How is the percentage calculated?

We compare your answers about living together with the other person’s, weighting each one by how much it matters to you. Then we add interests, area, budget, timing and age. The reasons are always next to the number.

Do I have to look for the right people myself?

We take care of it, and that’s the best part. We find the people most compatible with you, 70% or more both ways, and open the chat between you. Go in and it’s all there: who they are, what you agree on and what you don’t. If it’s not for you, end the connection with one tap.

Who sees my profile?

Only students using Uniflat in the city where you’re going to study, and only the ones we put you in touch with. You can pause your profile whenever you like: we stop connecting you with new people.
