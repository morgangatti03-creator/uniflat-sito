Site cookie notice

Last updated: 29 September 2026

What they are

Cookies are small files the site saves in your browser to remember something from one page to the next. On uniflat.it we use very few, and we tell you which.

The ones we use

Name What it's for How long Needs your consent?
uniflat_consenso Remembers whether you tapped "Accept" or "Decline", so we don't ask again on every page 6 months No, it's technical
ph_* Site statistics with PostHog: which pages are viewed, how many people tap Join, at which step of the form they stop 12 months Yes. It only starts if you tap "Accept"

Statistics use a random identifier. They don't contain your name or your email, even if you join the waiting list. The data stays in the European Union and we keep it for 30 days. PostHog Inc. is a US company: details are in the privacy notice, at uniflat.it/en/privacy.

We use no advertising or profiling cookies, and no third-party services that set them (no social buttons, no embedded videos, no fonts loaded from other sites).

How you choose

The first time you open the site, a notice appears at the bottom with two equal buttons, "Accept" and "Decline". Until you choose, nothing starts. The site works exactly the same either way, sign-up included.

To change your mind, tap "Cookie preferences" at the bottom of any page. If you switch from "Accept" to "Decline", statistics stop immediately and the ph_* cookies are deleted.

You can also delete cookies from your browser settings.
