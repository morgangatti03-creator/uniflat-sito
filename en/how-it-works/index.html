Safety, plainly.

Only people aged 18 and over can use Uniflat. We handle the rest.

  • You can block anyone, and blocking works in both directions.
  • You can report a profile or a message, and we read every report within 24 hours.
  • Photos are kept in a private store. Before upload your phone strips hidden data, including GPS location.
