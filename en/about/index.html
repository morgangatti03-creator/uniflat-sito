The problem

Today, looking for a place as a student from out of town works backwards. You find the room, you sign, and only when you move in do you learn who you’ll be living with.

What we do

Uniflat flips the order. First you find the people you get along with. Then you look for a place together, or move into a house where you already know who’s waiting.

University years are short, and they matter a lot.

Somewhere, someone is looking for a flatmate like you.

Leave your email and we’ll write to you on the day we open in your city. Once, and that’s it.
