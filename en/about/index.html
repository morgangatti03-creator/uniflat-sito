About us

We changed cities twice and houses four times.

We’re two students who moved away from home for university. We left to study, as so many young people do every year, and we ended up in the same situation as everyone else: a new city, a room found in a hurry, and on the other side of the wall people we hadn’t chosen.

In the first years it went wrong more than once. Opposite hours, different ideas about tidiness, guests and quiet, arguments that two questions asked beforehand would have avoided. It wasn’t anyone’s fault: nobody had simply asked us anything.

Then there were the other houses. The ones with flatmates who wait for you for dinner when you’re back late from the library, who go through your notes with you the night before an exam, who keep you afloat in the months when exams seem never to end. Many of them are our closest friends today.

The difference between those houses was never the flat. It was the people.

The problem

Today, looking for a place as a student from out of town works backwards. You find the room, you sign, and only when you move in do you learn who you’ll be living with. The landlord doesn’t tell you, listings don’t mention it, and Facebook groups are lists of rooms, not people.

What we do

Uniflat flips the order. First you find the people you get along with, and we tell you how compatible you are and why. Then you look for a place together, or move into a house where you already know who’s waiting. Looking for a home gets simpler, and better.

We do it because university years are short and they matter a lot. People who spend them with the right people carry them for the rest of their lives.
